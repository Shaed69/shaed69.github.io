ClipForge — Privacy Policy

ClipForge is a personal desktop application used solely by its owner to edit and upload their own video content to their own social media accounts.

Data collected: None. ClipForge has no server and collects no data from any user. OAuth tokens for the owner's own TikTok account are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never transmitted to any third party.

Data sharing: No data is shared, sold, or transferred to anyone.

Data deletion: Deleting the app's local cache folder removes all stored tokens. You may also revoke access at any time in TikTok's app settings.
